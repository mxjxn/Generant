Revision 4 — Veil / chord transitions

No lead melody. The drums, bass, original stabs and Afterimage carry the song.
Veil uses the existing four lower chord voices as a soft, dark FM pad, with
slow attacks, a little stereo detuning and a blurred room. It appears only
at selected handoffs and stays out of the main grooves and drop.

Bar 03       A quiet Gm9 swell in the introduction.
Bars 08, 24  Last-chord swells carry the change into the next section.
Bar 25       One soft chord at the breakdown entrance.
Bars 31–32   A swelling altered dominant, then three soft Dm9 chord pulses
             spaced a dotted eighth apart; silence before the drop.
Bars 48, 56  Chord swells into Soft landing and After hours.
Bars 58, 61  Sparse outro chords.
Bar 64       A final quiet Dm9 voicing with a release tail.

The chord-stab GVerb transitions are retained. The rejected lead, its pitch
modulator and its dedicated saturation/delay/room devices are removed.
Veil has two stored patches: 00 swell and 01 soft pulse. PR changes the
articulation before the pre-drop pulses, then resets it in the next pattern.

Harmony A: Dm9 → Bbmaj9(#11) → Gm9 → A7(b9,b13)
Harmony B: Fmaj9 → Em7b5(11) → A7(b9,b13) → Dm9
Bass carries the roots; the upper voicings are intentionally rootless.
Dotted eighth = 0.75 beats; ghost echoes = 0.375 beats.

Six instruments: Velvet stabs, Stab ghosts, Root bass, Veil transition chords,
Afterimage and 909 heel & toe. Two effects: Veil room and Velvet transition bloom.
Drums, harmony and accompaniment notes are unchanged from revision 3.
The previous version is in backup/sideways-bloom-hush-v3/ for comparison.
Velvet program slots: 00 dry / 01 open / 02 low light.

Drum source: your local Samples From Mars / Essential WAV From Mars
16 Bit / 909 From Mars library. Kick: Color 03 Short D 04;
clap blended with SD Clean E 03; CH Clean 01; Rim Clean.
